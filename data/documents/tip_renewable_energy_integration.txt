Renewable Energy Integration

Use solar electricity first for flexible daytime work. When forecasts show clear or partly cloudy conditions, schedule EV charging, water heating, laundry, and dishwashing near the middle of the day.

Cloud cover and solar radiation matter more than the weather label alone. A bright partly cloudy day can still provide useful solar power, while a dark overcast day may make low price overnight charging the better choice.

If export payments are low, using solar power in the home can be more valuable than sending it to the grid. Keep enough flexibility to shift non urgent tasks when the forecast changes.
