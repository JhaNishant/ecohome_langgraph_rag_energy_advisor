Seasonal Energy Management

Berlin households usually see more solar opportunity in late spring and summer, and more heating demand in autumn and winter. Review schedules at the beginning of each season rather than relying on one year round routine.

In winter, protect comfort first and look for small changes such as lower overnight heating, full appliance loads, and off peak charging. In summer, use shade, ventilation, and pre cooling to reduce late afternoon cooling demand.

Track weekly energy cost and solar generation. The pattern is more useful than a single unusual day.
