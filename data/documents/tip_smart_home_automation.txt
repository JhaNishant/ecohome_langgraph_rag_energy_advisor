Smart Home Automation Tips

Use delayed start, smart plugs, and home routines to move flexible work away from the evening peak. Good candidates include dishwashers, washing machines, dryers, pool pumps, and EV charging.

Set a rule that starts a flexible appliance only when it is full, the electricity price is low, or expected solar radiation is strong. Always keep safety limits in place: do not run appliances unattended when the manufacturer advises against it.

Review automation once a month. A routine that was useful in summer may need different hours in a darker winter month.
