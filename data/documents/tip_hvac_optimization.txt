HVAC Optimization for Berlin Homes

Use a programmable thermostat to reduce heating or cooling when rooms are empty. In winter, set a lower temperature overnight and warm the home shortly before people wake up. In summer, pre cool during lower cost hours if a hot afternoon is expected.

Keep filters clean, avoid blocking radiators, and close blinds on sunny summer afternoons. Small changes work best when they are paired with a comfortable temperature range rather than aggressive temperature swings.

For a heat pump, avoid repeatedly turning the system fully off and on. A steady, modest adjustment usually uses less energy and protects comfort.
